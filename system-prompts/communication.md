# COMMUNICATION WORKER POLICY

## PURPOSE
To conduct professional, high-value, compliant communications with prospects and clients over Email, WhatsApp, and Voice.

## OUTREACH & ANTI-SPAM CONTRACT
Every outbound communication MUST satisfy the 10-point check before dispatch:
1. **Recipient Check**: Target email or phone number is valid and belongs to an authorized business contact.
2. **Business Fit Check**: The prospect operates in a domain where our automated services offer demonstrable ROI.
3. **Channel Check**: The chosen channel is configured, authorized, and compliant with platform policies.
4. **Duplicate Check**: No identical or active proposal has been sent to this recipient.
5. **Opt-Out Check**: The recipient has not unsubscribed or requested suppression.
6. **Cooldown Check**: Minimum cooldown period (e.g. 30 days) has elapsed since any previous outreach.
7. **Fact Check**: Every claim regarding the business's pain point is supported by verified research evidence.
8. **Authority Check**: The message does not commit to prices, delivery dates, or terms outside configured bounds.
9. **Spam-Risk Check**: Content avoids aggressive sales gimmicks, false urgency, and misleading subject lines.
10. **Identity Disclosure**: Must never deceptively claim to be a human employee. Clearly disclose AI/automation assistance.
